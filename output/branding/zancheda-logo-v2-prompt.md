## 完整生成提示词

Use case: logo-brand.
Design a sophisticated, original Chinese product logo for “赞车搭”, a Youzan-related 3D automotive vinyl-wrap customization and sales-preview tool. Treat the two attached images ONLY as brand-language references: image 1 is the Youzan red thumbs-up symbol; image 2 is the parent-brand Chinese wordmark. The new product needs a distinctive original symbol plus its own exact three-character name. This is a fresh design direction, not a Z racing logo.

Strategic idea: “Give your car a look you love.” Customers try and combine colors, finishes and liveries, visually choose a personalized car, and share that selection. Three meanings must become ONE memorable silhouette: 赞 = approval and Youzan family connection; 车 = automobile; 搭 = applying and trying interchangeable wrap.

Specific visual concept: design an elegant solid red thumbs-up-inspired symbol with generous simple rounded contours, an upright soft thumb, a compact palm, and simplified hand anatomy. Within the lower palm, carve a generous WHITE negative-space compact side-view car silhouette, with a legible cabin/roof, short hood, and two simple wheel cues. This white car is the principal clever visual discovery, and must remain recognizable at small size. At the lower-right corner of the red hand, introduce ONE small flat folded-back vinyl-film corner: a simple red/pink geometric fold and white separator, integrated into the outer contour. This is the “try a wrap / mix and match” cue, not a separate sticker illustration. Make the result look natural and inevitable, not a collage of a hand, a car and a sticker. Use at most 3 solid vector shapes plus negative space. Strong flat vivid Youzan-family red sampled visually from the references, charcoal typography; one very restrained pale-red fold permitted. Design an original subbrand emblem using the parent’s visual vocabulary, not an unchanged official Youzan mark with a decoration pasted on.

Horizontal composition: left symbol; right exact text “赞车搭”. Chinese wordmark is refined, upright, generous and optically spaced, in an original medium-bold clean modern Chinese sans-serif with subtly softened corners. All strokes must read clearly. Less heavy than a car-tuning garage wordmark; friendly credible business-software brand. Symbol height approximately 1.25 times wordmark height. Thoughtful space between symbol and text. The artboard shows only one principal logo lockup at large scale and generous whitespace, nothing else.

Flat vector-style precision; entirely solid opaque pure white background #FFFFFF; sharp immaculate boundaries; no gradients, glow, shadows, 3D, metallic effects, lighting, embossed print, paper texture, arrows, racing stripes, shields, extra icons or watermarks. No red circle container, no app squircle container. No “有赞” wordmark, no English, no tagline, no explanatory text. Only text “赞车搭”, exact and legible. Landscape canvas with logo centered, the whole lockup comfortably within frame. This should feel like a conceptually intelligent brand designer’s considered identity rather than a generic automotive template.
