# Livery artwork and placement

## User-provided atlas

`kuromi-atlas.png` is an unmodified copy of the user-provided
`/Users/chenfeiyu/Downloads/68dc1c145042af05a62dc08da8887772.png` (2048 × 2048).
The user identified it as artwork from their benchmark configurator. No CC0 or
other public redistribution license is asserted for this character artwork.

The top two long panels map to opposite sides, with independent readable
orientations. The bottom-left panel is rotated in sampling coordinates for the
hood. Border pixels are excluded. Plain areas are sampled for the pink-to-black
body fill. Artwork placement is fitted to Car10, not a claim to reproduce the
benchmark's unavailable UV layout. This is a display mapping, not a print/cutting template.

## Original demo graphics

Classic racing stripes, side graphics, cream/orange/navy retro racing and
purple-blue flowing lines are original procedural graphics in `src/viewer/livery.ts`.
No brand logos or third-party racing skin files are included.
